3DPawsnToys — Pet Toy Safety & Product Information

Please read before giving the toy to your pet.

Safety Warning: Always supervise your pet during play. No pet toy is indestructible. Inspect regularly and discard if damaged — loose pieces may cause choking, dental injury or intestinal blockage.

What this toy is made of

Our toys are 3D printed from one of the following pet‑safe thermoplastics. The specific material used for your toy is marked on the packaging.

PLA (Polylactic Acid) — plant‑based, rigid. Best for light chewers and fetch toys.
PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol) — tougher, slightly flexible. Good for moderate chewers.
TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) — flexible and rubber‑like. Best for chewable balls and gentle tug toys.

All materials are non‑toxic and food‑contact safe in their raw form. They are not intended to be eaten. This toy is not a treat or food item.

Chew strength guidance

These toys are designed for light to moderate chewers.

They are not recommended for aggressive or "power" chewers — pets that routinely destroy rubber or nylon toys within minutes. For power chewers, please choose a commercial toy rated specifically for heavy chewing.

If you're unsure which category your pet falls into, start with short supervised play sessions and inspect the toy afterwards.

Size guidance

Always choose a toy that is larger than your pet can fit fully inside its mouth. A toy small enough to be swallowed whole is a choking and intestinal blockage risk, regardless of material.

If your pet grows, or you buy for a larger pet, re‑check the size.

Supervision & inspection

  • Always supervise your pet during play with this toy.
  • Inspect the toy before and after every play session.
  • Discard the toy immediately if it becomes cracked, chipped, broken, or chewed into small pieces.
  • Loose fragments may cause choking, dental damage, or intestinal obstruction, which can be life‑threatening.
  • Do not allow your pet to ingest any part of this toy. If ingestion is suspected, contact your veterinarian immediately.

Not suitable for children

This is a pet toy, not a human toy. Keep away from children under 3 years of age due to small parts and choking hazard. Not edible.

Cleaning & care

  • Clean with mild soap and warm water.
  • Do not microwave, boil, put in a dishwasher, or expose to open flame.
  • Store away from direct sunlight and extreme heat — PLA in particular can soften above ~55 °C / 130 °F.
